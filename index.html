// ABOUT ME

I spent a good part of my life moving around as a military brat, including living in Germany and the Philippines. Before I ever figured out where I was headed, I worked through a lifetime of varied hands-on experiences. I served in the U.S. Air Force until 1993 as an avionics repair technician working on LANTIRN navigation and targeting systems for F-15 and F-16 aircraft, followed by 15 years turning wrenches as a diesel mechanic.

My journey with technology started way back in the 1980s wiring up mechanical keyboards on a Sinclair ZX81, programming in GW-BASIC and Pascal, and writing MS-DOS batch files. Today, at 53, I’m channeling decades of real-world troubleshooting into full-time study for my Associate of Applied Science in Computer and Information Systems at Dyersburg State.
